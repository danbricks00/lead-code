About LeadBot

Connecting customers with qualified tradesmen through intelligent lead generation

Our Story

LeadBot was founded with a simple mission: to bridge the gap between customers seeking quality tradesmen and skilled professionals looking for work. We recognized that finding reliable tradesmen for home improvement projects was often a frustrating and time-consuming process.

Our platform uses intelligent chatbot technology to gather detailed project requirements and match customers with qualified tradesmen in their area. We've helped thousands of homeowners find the perfect professionals for their projects, from small repairs to major renovations.
