Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

How does Kiwi Underfloor Heating work?

We use an AI‑assisted chatbot to collect just the essential details and match you with the right installer. Faster matching and lower overheads mean more competitive quotes.

How much does it cost?

It’s free to request a quote. Our automated process reduces admin costs so installers can price more competitively.

How do I register as a tradesman?

Simply click the "Tradesman Login" button and sign in with your Google account. Complete your registration by providing your trade details, business information, and service areas.

What types of trades do you support?

We support all major trades including builders, electricians, plumbers, heating engineers, painters, roofers, landscapers, and many more. If you don't see your trade listed, contact us!

How quickly will I receive leads?

Leads are sent instantly when they match your criteria. Most tradesmen receive their first lead within 24 hours of registration, depending on demand in their area.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely! We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect all customer and tradesman data. Your information is never shared with third parties without your consent.
