Frequently Asked Questions

How does LeadBot work?
LeadBot uses intelligent chatbot technology to gather your project requirements and match you with qualified tradesmen in your area. Simply start a conversation and we'll guide you through the process.
Are the tradesmen verified?
Yes, all tradesmen on our platform are thoroughly vetted and verified. We check their qualifications, insurance, and previous work to ensure quality service.
What services do you cover?
We cover all major trades including building/construction, electrical, plumbing, and many other services. If you need a specific trade, just ask!
How quickly will I get matched?
Most customers receive their first quote within 24 hours. Our system works quickly to connect you with available tradesmen in your area.
