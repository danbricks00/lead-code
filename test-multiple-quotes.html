📋 Scenario: Multiple Tradesmen Quotes

This test demonstrates what happens when multiple tradesmen (A, B, C) submit quotes for the same job, and the customer accepts one of them:

  • Tradesman A: Quote accepted by customer → Status: "Accepted"
  • Tradesman B: Quote not selected → Status: "Job Awarded to Another Tradesman"
  • Tradesman C: Quote not selected → Status: "Job Awarded to Another Tradesman"
👷 Tradesman A (Will be Accepted)
👷 Tradesman B (Will be Awarded to Another)
👷 Tradesman C (Will be Awarded to Another)
